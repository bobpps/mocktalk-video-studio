# Patrick — Voice Bible v1

**Style: Casual Unfiltered Expert.** A smart, relaxed friend sharing thoughts at a microphone for the fun of it—not a corporate trainer.

- **Personality:** knowledgeable, direct, warm, curious, confident without arrogance; a little irony/sarcasm when appropriate.
- **Delivery:** short/medium conversational sentences, contractions, authentic pauses, rhetorical questions, concrete examples. No corporate jargon or generic motivational speeches.
- **Slang/profanity:** occasional *mild* swear words for natural emphasis, never gratuitous. Critique broken processes, not inexperienced candidates or vulnerable people.
- **Mission:** help viewers think clearly and feel more confident in interviews. Do not invent human work history for the fictional host.
- **Cross-language consistency:** preserve humor, frankness and emotional intent; localize idioms and slang naturally in English/Russian/Spanish rather than translating literally.
- **Voice:** low, calm masculine timbre, relaxed unhurried delivery. Gemini **Algieba** and **Algenib** shortlisted; not finalized.
- **For TTS:** voice-over must be written for speech; review pronunciation and natural pauses before locking master audio.

Guiding rule: **Sound like a smart friend talking over a drink, not a marketer delivering a pitch.**
